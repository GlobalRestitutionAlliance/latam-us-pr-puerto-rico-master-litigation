# Master Federal Court Complaint - Puerto Rico

**Plaintiff:** People of Puerto Rico
**Defendant:** United States; Congress; PROMESA Board; FEMA
**Jurisdiction:** 28 USC 1331, 1343, 1361; Territorial Clause; ICERD; ICCPR

## Causes of Action
1. **Denial of Self-Determination** — No status resolution since 1898
2. **Political Disenfranchisement** — No presidential vote, no voting Congress member
3. **PROMESA Violation** — Unelected fiscal board supersedes elected government
4. **Economic Colonization** — §936 repeal, $74B debt, austerity, depopulation
5. **Disaster Discrimination** — Maria/Fiona response vs. states
6. **Environmental Injustice** — Coal ash, Superfund sites, military contamination

## Historical Arc: 1898-2026
## Judgment: 1898 Spanish-American War, 1917 Jones Act (Citizenship), 1948 Gag Law, 1952 Commonwealth, 1996 §936 Repeal, 2016 PROMESA, 2017 Maria, No Vote, 3.2M US Citizens
## Pool: $100.00B

## Demands
A. Binding status plebiscite (Statehood/Independence/Free Association)
B. PROMESA repeal / board dissolution
C. Debt audit and cancellation
C. Equitable disaster funding (Stafford Act parity)
E. Environmental remediation (Vieques, coal ash, military sites)
F. Reparations for 126 years of territorial subordination
